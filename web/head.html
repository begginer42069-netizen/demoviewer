Strafes (kz_jump_stats rules)

eff (kz_jump_stats) = frames with a speed gain ÷ frames the key was held; on overlap frames only the newest key can score the gain. avg eff (max) (GC) = your mouse turn compared with the ideal turn for your speed, averaged over the strafe, and its best frame; over 100% means turning faster than ideal (ducking lowers the ideal turn a lot). GC sometimes splits strafes a frame differently, so averages can differ from GC's by a few %.

What-if take-off — same strafes, different pre / jumpoff

Only the take-off changes: speed is set to your pre (same direction) and the take-off point is moved so the jumpoff matches. Every input from the jump on is the player's own. Nothing before the jump is simulated.
FOG: jump on an earlier ground frame (1 = the first frame on the ground). Pre and jumpoff then fill in with what you really had on that frame — change them further if you like. FOG can't go above what you actually did.

Accuracy vs kz_jump_stats on test demos: distance ±0.02, jumpoff ±0.01, landing/lasting ±0.02, block exact, prestrafe ±0.06 (demos store speed in 1/8 u/s steps), strafes/efficiency usually within a few %. Movable blocks (e.g. kz_longjumparena) can't be measured from the map file.
